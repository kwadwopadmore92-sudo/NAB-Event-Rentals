About NAB

We handle the pieces,
so you can host.

NAB Event Rentals started with one simple frustration: event organizers spend more time chasing rental vendors than planning the event itself. We built a single source for the decor layer — florals down to the last charger plate — so planners get one delivery, one point of contact, and one pickup.

For planners

Built around your timeline

We work to your run-of-show — setting before vendor call time and clearing out on your schedule, not ours.

For corporate teams

Consistent, on-brand setups

Repeatable table setups across multi-day conferences and recurring company events, with the same inventory each time.

For everyone

Nothing to return

We deliver, style, and collect. You focus on the guests, not on repacking crates at midnight.

What we believe

Decor should disappear into the moment.

The best tablescape is the one no one has to think about — it just feels right for the room. That's the standard we hold every linen fold and place setting to, whether it's a 60-guest wedding or a 400-seat conference dinner.
